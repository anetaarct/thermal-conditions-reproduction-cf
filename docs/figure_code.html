1 Thermal-window figures

The manuscript’s three-panel environmental figure uses the Gaussian location–scale fit to annual mean temperature; its third panel shows the separate within-window daily-SD OLS fit. Run R/25_environment_temperature_location_scale.R with glmmTMB 1.1.14. Gaussian dispersion is log SD, not log variance. The earlier two OLS plots are archived alternatives rather than the main environmental analysis.
