1 Female age class

Female age class comes exclusively from FAGE_CAT. Its two categories are YOUNG and OLD; no other age field is used in the analyses.

2 Relative laying date

Relative laying date is centred within breeding season using the annual median in the validated reproductive dataset:

\[ \mathrm{rel\_LD}_{iy}=\mathrm{LD}_{iy}-\mathrm{median}(\mathrm{LD}_{y}). \]

After outcome-, age-, and complete-case restrictions define a model-specific analytical sample, rel_LD is recalculated in that final sample by subtracting its annual median. The annual median is therefore exactly zero in every included season. Model-specific checks of the annual mean, annual median, and association with window temperature are reported in the corresponding analysis chapters.

3 Inclusion criteria and sample reconciliation

Records are eligible when clutch size is 2–9 eggs. Fledging records additionally require fledgling number to be between zero and clutch size. Broods with zero fledglings are retained. Missing fledgling counts do not exclude a record from the clutch-size analysis.
